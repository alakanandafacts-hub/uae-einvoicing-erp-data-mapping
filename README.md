UAE eInvoicing ERP Data Mapping Checklist

This resource helps businesses prepare their ERP data before connecting to a UAE eInvoicing solution or Accredited Service Provider (ASP).

1. Customer and supplier records
- Check that customer and supplier names are consistent.
- Review tax registration numbers where applicable.
- Validate billing addresses and required identification fields.
- Remove duplicate or outdated records.

2. Invoice data
- Review invoice numbering and document dates.
- Check item descriptions, quantities, units and prices.
- Validate discounts, taxable amounts and VAT calculations.
- Confirm credit note and adjustment processes.

3. Tax information
- Review VAT registration details and applicable tax categories.
- Check that tax codes map correctly between the ERP and the intended eInvoicing solution.
- Confirm that finance teams can review exceptions.

4. Data quality and integration
- Identify missing mandatory fields.
- Document how ERP fields map to the target integration format.
- Test sample invoices before production deployment.
- Define procedures for rejected documents and error correction.

5. Implementation readiness
- Assign an owner for data validation.
- Document the approval workflow.
- Keep a record of test results and outstanding issues.
- Confirm applicable UAE requirements with an appropriately qualified adviser or ASP.
